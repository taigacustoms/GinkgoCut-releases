<h1 align="center">GinkgoCut</h1>

**Vinyl cutting software for print shops** (Windows 10 and 11).

Design decals, signs and heat transfer vinyl, then cut them one color at a
time on your cutter. Part of the same suite as the ShokujoDX embroidery
program, with the same ribbon and window layout.

is new and not signed yet: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## What it does

- **New Design**: pick the machine, roll or sheet on a mat, the width, the
  feed edge (top or bottom) and the vinyl; the choices stay for next time.
- **Design**: shapes, pen, node editing, text with weld and text on an arc,
  import of SVG, PDF, AI, DXF, PLT, EMF and WMF, picture trace for logos,
  simplify, weld, subtract, contour, nest, step and repeat, rulers and guides.
- **Materials**: a library of Oracal and Siser adhesive and heat transfer
  vinyl with recommended settings, and your own saved settings for each
  machine.
- **Cutting**: HPGL (USCutter, Vevor and others), Roland, Graphtec and
  Silhouette cutters; Cricut through a Design Space file. Inside-first cut
  order, overcut, blade offset, weed boxes and weed lines, mirror for HTV,
  tiles for large jobs, cut preview and test cut.
- **Shop tools**: print and cut marks, job sheets for the operator with the
  layout, quantities and machine settings, and quotes.

## Before the first real job

Make a **test cut** on scrap vinyl on each machine (Cut > Test Cut). The
recommended material settings are starting points: save the settings that
work for each material on each machine. Check heat press settings against the
current sheet of the vinyl maker.
